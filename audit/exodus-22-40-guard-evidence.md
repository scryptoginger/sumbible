### Tabernacle repetition (25-31 vs 35-40) — primary-text discipline

The construction chapters are NOT a shortened recap of the instructions — they have their own primary text and theological emphasis (the OBEDIENT FULFILLMENT of the divine pattern). The risk is memory-shortcut: paraphrasing 35-40 too closely to 25-31 because the agent has just drafted them.
