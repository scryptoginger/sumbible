The chapter has three major movements: the LORD's address to Joshua now old and stricken in years with the framework's "there remaineth yet very much land to be possessed" register (13:1-7); the eastern tribes' inheritance review recalling Moses' prior trans-Jordan allotment (13:8-14); the specific trans-Jordan tribal-territorial allotments for Reuben, Gad, and half-Manasseh + the structural-reminder that Levi has no territorial inheritance (13:15-33).

**The eastern tribes' inheritance review** (13:8-14). The chapter then installs the framework's structural-reminder of the trans-Jordan allotment Moses had granted. <VerseRef book="joshua" chapter={13} verse="8" /> registers the framework: the Reubenites, the Gadites, and the half-tribe of Manasseh have already received their inheritance, "which Moses gave them, beyond Jordan eastward, even as Moses the servant of the LORD gave them." The framework's two-fold installation within the chapter (13:14 + 13:33) registers the framework as the allotment-block's structural-bookend boundary-condition — the framework's territorial-distribution operates within Levi-not-included from opening to close.
