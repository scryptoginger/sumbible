Joshua 9 narrates the Gibeonite deception and the covenant that held despite the deception. The chapter has four major movements: the west-Jordan kings unite against Israel (9:1-2); the Gibeonites' deception with moldy bread and worn garments + the covenant sworn without consulting the LORD (9:3-15); the deception's discovery + the oath upheld + the Gibeonites become hewers of wood and drawers of water (9:16-21); Joshua's address to the Gibeonites + their service (9:22-27).

**The west-Jordan kings unite** (9:1-2). The chapter opens at <VerseRef book="joshua" chapter={9} verse="1-2" /> with the narrative-pivot from the Gerizim-Ebal ceremony at the prior chapter: the kings of the Hittites, Amorites, Canaanites, Perizzites, Hivites, and Jebusites gather "to fight with Joshua and with Israel, with one accord." The framework's narrative-register: the coalition-formation operates as the conquest-narrative's structural-development after Israel's two-fold defeat of Jericho and Ai.
