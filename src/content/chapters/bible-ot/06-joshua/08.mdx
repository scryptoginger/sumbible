Joshua 8 has two major sections operating at distinct registers: the Ai-narrative (8:1-29) executing the LORD's ambush instructions and concluding with the king of Ai hanged on a tree per the Deut 21:22-23 framework; the Gerizim-Ebal covenant-ceremony (8:30-35) executing Deut 27's foreshadowed instruction and Deut 31's law-deposit + public-reading framework. **The Ai-narrative** (8:1-29). The chapter opens at <VerseRef book="joshua" chapter={8} verse={1} /> with the LORD's reassurance after the Josh 7 defeat: "Fear not, neither be thou dismayed: take all the people of war with thee, and arise, go up to Ai." The framework's structural-register: the LORD's chazaq-ve-amets framework (the formula Session 20's Deut 31 + Session 21's Josh 1 LangNote installed across the leadership-succession framework) recurs at the chapter's opening — the framework operates not only at the succession-moment but at each crisis-moment requiring re-commissioning.

The chapter narrates the ambush at 8:2-23: Joshua positions 30,000 warriors west of Ai; the main force feigns retreat; the city's defenders pursue; the ambush force seizes and burns the city; the defenders are caught between the burning city and Joshua's returning force. The framework's military-narrative-register registers as Joshua's first independent strategic command — the chapter at hand's narrative-substance is Joshua's growing-into-the-Mosaic-succession framework's leader-register.

The chapter then installs the framework's first cross-session connection-back at <VerseRef book="joshua" chapter={8} verse={29} />: "And the king of Ai he hanged on a tree until eventide: and as soon as the sun was down, Joshua commanded that they should take his carcase down from the tree, and cast it at the entering of the gate of the city, and raise thereon a great heap of stones." **The Gerizim-Ebal covenant-ceremony** (8:30-35). The chapter then installs its structural-climax — the execution of the covenant-ceremony Moses had commanded.
