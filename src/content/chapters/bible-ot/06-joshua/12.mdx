Joshua 12 is the conquest-narrative's structural-closing inventory + Part I of Joshua's structural-closing chapter. The chapter has two major sections operating at distinct registers: the trans-Jordan section (12:1-6) enumerates the two kings defeated under Moses east of the Jordan; the cis-Jordan section (12:7-24) enumerates the thirty-one kings defeated under Joshua's leadership west of the Jordan. The chapter's form-as-substance discipline registers the numerical comprehensiveness as the framework's theological point — the framework's full king-by-king enumeration installs the conquest-narrative's structural-completion.

**The trans-Jordan section** (12:1-6). The chapter opens at <VerseRef book="joshua" chapter={12} verse={1} />: "Now these are the kings of the land, which the children of Israel smote, and possessed their land on the other side Jordan toward the rising of the sun, from the river Arnon unto mount Hermon, and all the plain on the east." The framework's structural-elements: (a) the geographic-comprehensive register (from Arnon to Hermon — the trans-Jordan's full south-to-north extent); (b) the eastern-side-of-the-Jordan framework registers the section as the Pentateuchal-conquest's structural-summary.

The chapter then names the two trans-Jordan kings at <VerseRef book="joshua" chapter={12} verse="2-5" />: Sihon king of the Amorites who dwelt in Heshbon + Og king of Bashan who dwelt at Ashtaroth and Edrei. The framework grounds the two-king framework in the prior Pentateuchal narrative — Sihon's defeat at <VerseRef book="numbers" chapter={21} verse="21-32" /> (drafted Session 14); Og's defeat at <VerseRef book="numbers" chapter={21} verse="33-35" /> + <VerseRef book="deuteronomy" chapter={3} verse="1-11" /> (drafted Sessions 14 + 16). The territory's distribution to Reuben + Gad + half-Manasseh at <VerseRef book="joshua" chapter={12} verse={6} /> recalls <VerseRef book="numbers" chapter={32} verse="1-42" /> (drafted Session 15) + <VerseRef book="deuteronomy" chapter={3} verse="12-20" /> (drafted Session 16).

The framework's structural-significance: the trans-Jordan section registers the Pentateuch's eastern-conquest framework alongside Joshua's western-conquest framework as one narrative-comprehensive conquest-arc spanning both bodies of canonical text. The chapter at hand is the OT-narrative's structural-summary of the two-stage conquest-arc — Moses' eastern-conquest + Joshua's western-conquest composed as one framework-completion.

**The cis-Jordan section** (12:7-24). The chapter then installs its structural-load: the thirty-one-king enumeration of the western-conquest's defeated rulers. <VerseRef book="joshua" chapter={12} verse="7" /> opens the section: "And these are the kings of the country which Joshua and the children of Israel smote on this side Jordan on the west, from Baalgad in the valley of Lebanon even unto the mount Halak, that goeth up to Seir." The framework's geographic-comprehensive register: from Baalgad in the north to Mount Halak going up to Seir in the south.

The chapter then enumerates the thirty-one kings city by city. The framework's enumeration follows roughly the conquest-narrative's sequence — Jericho (12:9, recalling Josh 6), Ai (12:9, recalling Josh 8), Jerusalem (12:10, the king of the southern-coalition), Hebron + Jarmuth + Lachish + Eglon (12:10-12, the southern-coalition kings of Josh 10), Gezer + Debir + Geder + Hormah + Arad + Libnah + Adullam + Makkedah + Bethel + Tappuah + Hepher + Aphek + Lasharon + Madon + Hazor (12:12-19, mixing southern + central + northern), Shimronmeron + Achshaph + Taanach + Megiddo + Kedesh + Jokneam of Carmel + Dor + Goiim of Gilgal + Tirzah (12:20-24).
